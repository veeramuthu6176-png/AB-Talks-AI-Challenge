Key Learnings from Day 12
1. AI can personalise at scale
Claude didn't generate generic templates — it tailored every section to my
specific profile, certifications, university, and target role. The output
was ready to use with minimal edits.
2. Personal branding starts before your first job
I learned that as a student, I already have a unique value proposition.
My AI + Analytics dual exposure is a differentiator most freshers don't have.
3. The LinkedIn headline matters more than I thought
Recruiters spend ~6 seconds on a profile. A strong, keyword-rich headline
is the first filter. Claude generated one I can use immediately.
4. Skill gaps are opportunities, not weaknesses
The gap analysis showed me exactly what to learn next and in what order.
Having a clear roadmap (SQL → BI Tools → Portfolio → Apply) removes
the paralysis of not knowing where to start.
5. A cover letter and a cold email are very different things
Claude taught me that each communication format has a different purpose:
Cover Letter → formal, ATS-optimised, submitted with application
Recruiter Email → punchy, scannable, focused on getting a reply
Hiring Manager Email → trust-building, shows research and interest
6. Follow-up is not optional
Most students give up after one message. The follow-up email template
reminded me that persistence (done politely) is part of the process.
7. The STAR method is the interview foundation
Claude structured my interview talking points around Situation, Task,
Action, Result — a framework I can now apply to every question I prepare.
