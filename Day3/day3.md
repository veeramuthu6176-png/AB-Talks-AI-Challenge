# Day 3 – Role-Based Prompting

## What I Learned
Role-Based Prompting means instructing Claude to assume a specific role or persona before asking a question. It is also called Persona Prompting or Act-As Prompting.

## Without Role Prompt
**Prompt:** "How do I handle a team conflict?"

**Output:**
Generic tips: "Talk it out, listen to both sides, find common ground..."

## With Founder Persona
**Prompt:** "Act as a Founder. How do I handle a team conflict?"

**Output:**
Focus on company culture, team alignment, and long-term vision. Address conflict early before it affects productivity and investor confidence.

## With Developer Persona
**Prompt:** "Act as a Senior Developer. How do I handle a team conflict?"

**Output:**
Use structured communication like async updates, clear task ownership in GitHub/Jira, and code review norms to reduce friction points.

## Comparison
| Type | Response Quality |
|------|-----------------|
| No Role | Generic, surface-level |
| Founder Persona | Strategic, culture-focused |
| Developer Persona | Technical, process-oriented |

## Key Takeaways
- Same question + different role = completely different answer
- Role-Based Prompting shapes tone, style, and depth
- It is one of the most powerful beginner prompting techniques
