First 100 Users Plan
Run 15–20 discovery calls with NRI/urban adult children to pressure-test willingness to pay before building.
Sign 1–2 pilot pharmacy partners in a Tier 2/3 Tamil Nadu town as the primary distribution wedge.
Launch WhatsApp-based Wizard-of-Oz version to onboard first 20–30 households manually.

VERDICT: VALIDATE — Promising Problem, Unproven Business
The pain point is real, severe, and emotionally urgent, and the model correctly targets the paying customer (adult child) rather than the end user (elderly patient). But zero validation exists on willingness-to-pay, OCR accuracy on handwriting, or elderly adoption — and ARPU is low enough that this needs either B2B/clinic scale or a much larger household base to become venture-sustainable. Run the 30-day discovery and pilot sprint before committing to full build.
