🏁 Final Verdict
⚠️ Apply with Caution
This role has learning potential but carries significant red flags around compensation transparency and scope overload. Proceed only after clarifying stipend, mentorship availability, and actual day-to-day responsibilities.
💬 5 Smart Interview Questions
"Can you clarify how the performance-based stipend is calculated — what metrics determine the payout and what is the expected range?"
Tests: Salary transparency, fairness
"What does a typical week look like for this role — which of the listed tools would I use most frequently?"
Tests: Whether the JD is realistic or inflated
"Will I have a dedicated mentor or team lead to guide me during the initial months?"
Tests: Whether "self-starter" means abandoned
"How many people are currently on the data team, and how is work distributed?"
Tests: Team size and whether I'd be the only analyst
"What would success look like in the first 90 days, and what projects would I work on?"
Tests: Role clarity and growth opportunity
🧠 Key Learnings from Day 14
Red Flag Detection is a real-world prompt engineering use case — not just a classroom exercise
The R-C-I-F-S framework structures the AI's role clearly: Act as a Red Flag Detector → Analyze JD → Output structured risk report
"Performance-based stipend" is one of the biggest red flags in Indian startup job postings for freshers
A missing salary = a missing respect signal — always ask before accepting
Even a positive-sounding JD can hide exploitation in phrases like "wear many hats" or "fast-paced"
