1. Executive Summary
PocketKhata proposes a WhatsApp-native bookkeeping assistant for India's estimated 13–14 million kirana stores,
most of which still track sales, expenses, and customer credit on paper or by memory. The core insight is that these
owners already live inside WhatsApp — so the product should meet them there rather than asking them to learn a
new app.
The problem is real and well-documented at the market level, but currently unvalidated at the founder level: no
interviews, waitlist signups, or prototype usage exist yet. Founder-market fit is a genuine strength (direct family
exposure to the problem), which partially offsets the lack of formal validation.

13. Pivot Opportunities
● Narrow further: focus only on customer credit (udhaar) tracking — the single highest-pain feature — before
expanding to full bookkeeping.
● Shift buyer: sell a lender-facing "credit-readiness score" product to microfinance institutions using aggregated,
consented shop data.
● Adjacent segment: street vendors and small service providers (tailors, salons) with nearly identical informal
credit/tracking problems.
● B2B2C model: partner with FMCG distributors who already visit these shops, bundling the tool into their existing
relationship.

15. 30-Day Action Plan
Days Action Items
1–5 Write a 5-question interview script. Visit or call 15–20 kirana owners in your local area. Focus
on listening, not pitching.
6–10 Synthesize interviews: tag pain frequency, current workarounds, and any pricing signals.
Identify your strongest 3 quotes/insights.
11–15 Run a "Wizard of Oz" pilot: manually operate a WhatsApp number where you personally log
5–10 shops' data by hand, no code required.
16–20 Track daily usage/retention of the manual pilot. Ask pilot users directly what they'd pay and
what almost made them quit.
21–25 Decide feature scope for a true MVP based on pilot learnings (likely: credit tracking + daily
summary only).
26–30 If signal is strong: scope a minimal WhatsApp-API-based MVP with a developer/no-code tool.
If signal is weak: revisit Section 13 pivots before any build.
Final Reminder
The biggest risk to this idea is not competition — it's building before validating. Every task above can be
done with a phone and a notebook, at zero cost, before writing any code.
